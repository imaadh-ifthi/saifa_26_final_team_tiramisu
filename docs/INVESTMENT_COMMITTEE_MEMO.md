# Investment Committee Memo: Serendib University Endowment, $10M mandate

*Data to 2026-10-20. Team Tiramisu. All numbers are for the current allocation.*

## Recommendation: ACCEPT the allocation, but ADJUST the stress-loss limit

day-to-day risk is comfortably inside the limit, but a repeat of the 2008 Global Financial Crisis would lose 24.8%, above the 20% tolerance. No mix allowed by the mandate gets below 24.3% (bonds and gold are already at their caps), so the honest fix is the limit, not the portfolio.

## The portfolio

45% US bonds (AGG), 15% gold (GLD) and 40% stocks (SPY 23%, EFA 10%, XLK 6%, XLE 1%). It meets every mandate limit.

## How much can we lose tomorrow?

* On a bad day (the worst 1 in 40) we'd lose about **$130,656** on average. That's 65% of our $200,000 daily limit.
* On the worst 1-in-100 day: at least $128,070, and $158,843 on average when it happens.
* Can we trust that? The risk model was picked using 2011-2017 only, then tested once on 2018-2026: 26 days lost more than the 1-in-100 line, against 22 expected (old model: 26). Across all of 2020 it had 5 such days against 8 for the old model; in the crash itself (20 Feb-30 Apr) both had 3. No daily model avoids every COVID breach; regulators excluded 6-27 March 2020 for banks.

## Where the risk comes from

| Asset | Share of the money | Share of the tail risk (ES) |
|---|---:|---:|
| SPY | 23% | 31% |
| GLD | 15% | 28% |
| XLK | 6% | 16% |

* Gold is not acting as a safe asset right now: its correlation with stocks is 0.32 (normally 0.08), so it adds to the losses on bad days instead of cushioning them.
* Bonds still cushion, but less than usual: SPY-AGG correlation is 0.19. In March 2020 this flipped and bonds fell with stocks.
* Over budget (more than 2x its weight in tail risk): XLK (6% of money, 16% of risk).

## Stress tests (today's weights)

| Scenario | Loss, peak to trough | In dollars |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2008 Global Financial Crisis | 24.8% | $2,483,875 |
| March 2020 COVID crash | 16.6% | $1,655,075 |
| 2022 rate-hiking drawdown | 18.0% | $1,798,795 |
| Hedge failure: dash for cash 2.0 | 13.2% | $1,323,085 |

The last line is our own scenario: stocks crash while bonds and gold are sold for cash at the same time. Reverse stress test: the S&P 500 would have to fall about **48%** before we lose 20%.

## What we ask the board to approve

1. Raise the stress-loss limit from 20% to **26%**, peak to trough. The allocation already holds the most bonds and gold the mandate allows, and a 2008 repeat would still cost 24.8%.
2. Trim XLK by 5% into SPY: tail risk (ES) falls by $8,014 a day, expected return changes by -0.18 pp a year.
3. Daily limit: 1-day 97.5% ES at most $200,000, checked every morning.
4. Early warning: if the 60-day SPY-AGG correlation turns positive, review the bond allocation within a week.

*Caveats: the 2% daily and 20% stress limits are our assumptions until the client brief confirms them. The 2008 replay uses weights fitted on data that includes 2008. Stress tests show what could happen, not what will.*
